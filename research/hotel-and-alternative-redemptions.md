# Hotels + Alternative Redemptions for MR and UR

Research date: 2026-09-29. All point values and charts dated. Programs devalue often; reverify before booking.

Cardholder profile: Amex Platinum (Membership Rewards / MR) + Chase Sapphire Reserve (Ultimate Rewards / UR). SF Bay Area (SFO/SJC). Airline anchors already chosen: Avianca LifeMiles (Star Alliance), British Airways / Iberia Avios (Oneworld), Air France-KLM Flying Blue (SkyTeam).

---

## Verdict (read this first)

- **Single best use of UR: transfer 1:1 to World of Hyatt.** The user holds a **Chase Sapphire Reserve**, which **keeps the 1:1 UR->Hyatt ratio** after the October 1, 2026 devaluation that cut non-Reserve cards to 4:3. Hyatt is the only mainstream hotel program that reliably returns ~1.5-2.0c per point on a fixed(ish) award chart, and UR can only reach it through Chase. Runner-up for UR: transfer to airline partners for premium-cabin flights (LifeMiles/Avios/Flying Blue), which can exceed 2c and often 4-8c in business/first.
- **Single best use of MR: transfer to airline partners for premium cabins** (LifeMiles, Flying Blue, Avios via British Airways/Iberia/Aer Lingus). MR does **not** reach Hyatt. For hotels, MR's only strong play is **Hilton at 1:2** paired with Amex Platinum's Hilton Gold (free breakfast + 5th-night-free on award stays); Marriott (1:1) and Choice (1:1) are usually mediocre.
- **Where hotels rank:** UR->Hyatt is a top-tier redemption (roughly tied with, or just behind, premium-cabin flights). MR->hotels is mid-tier at best (Hilton) and otherwise poor. Every non-transfer path (portals, statement credits, gift cards, Amazon/PayPal checkout, cash) is worse and should be treated as a floor.
- **Portal floors to beat:** CSR redeems in Chase Travel at **1.5c** (if you applied before June 23, 2025) or **1.0c** (after), up to **2.0c** on "Points Boost" bookings. Amex Pay With Points is **1.0c** for flights, ~**0.7c** for prepaid hotels, ~**0.6c** cash. Transfer to Hyatt/airlines beats all of these.

### 1c. Marriott and IHG (for comparison)

**Marriott Bonvoy** — fully **dynamic pricing**; Marriott has not published a fixed off-peak/standard/peak category chart since retiring it (last fixed pricing removed March 2022, fully dynamic since 2023). Typical value ~**0.8c**, premium redemptions up to ~1.2c. Because UR and MR both transfer 1:1, and points are worth less than Hyatt's, Marriott transfers are **rarely a good trade** except to complete a Free Night Award top-off or when a specific property prices well. Source: [FrequentMiler Marriott guide](https://frequentmiler.com/marriott-bonvoy-complete-guide/), [UpgradedPoints dynamic-pricing coverage](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/marriott-bonvoy-dynamic-pricing-award-chart-removal/).

**IHG One Rewards** — also **dynamic**, roughly 10,000 to 100,000+ points/night (some >200k). Typical value ~**0.5-0.6c**, the weakest of the three. UR transfers 1:1. Source: [FrequentMiler IHG guide](https://frequentmiler.com/ihg-rewards-complete-guide/).

### 1d. Sweet-spot mechanics

- **Fifth-night-free on award stays:** Offered by **Marriott** and **Hilton** (5 nights for the points of 4). **Hilton Gold from Amex Platinum triggers this**, so MR->Hilton stays of 5 nights get an effective 20% discount. Sources: [UpgradedPoints 4th/5th-night guide](https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/hotels/fourth-or-fifth-night-free/), Amex Platinum Hilton Gold benefits below.
- **Fourth-night-free (IHG):** Offered on award stays, but **only to IHG co-branded cardholders** (Premier / Premier Business / Traveler). The user holds neither, so this does **not** apply. Source: [FrequentMiler IHG guide](https://frequentmiler.com/ihg-rewards-complete-guide/).
- **Hyatt has NO 4th/5th-night-free.** Multiple guides confirm Hyatt does not offer a consecutive-night free benefit. (One third-party chart page claimed a "5th night free"; the detailed program guides contradict it — treat Hyatt as having none.) Source: [FrequentMiler Hyatt guide](https://frequentmiler.com/world-of-hyatt-complete-guide/).
- **Hyatt Points + Cash:** Half the standard points plus a cash co-pay; resort/destination fees are **not** waived, which usually makes straight points or straight cash better. Source: [FrequentMiler Hyatt guide](https://frequentmiler.com/world-of-hyatt-complete-guide/).
- **Category / off-peak sweet spots (Hyatt):** Off-peak (or "Lowest" band) at Cat 1-4 properties is where cents-per-point spikes; all-inclusive Hyatt resorts and top Park Hyatts are the classic high-value targets.
- **Marriott Free Night Awards:** Co-brand FNAs cap at 85,000 points and can be topped off with up to 25,000 points — a way to squeeze more out of certificates, not a points-transfer play.

### 1e. Elite status the cards already grant (confirm current)

- **Amex Platinum -> Marriott Bonvoy Gold** (complimentary via enrollment): 25% bonus points, space-available room upgrades, 2pm late checkout, welcome gift points. No free breakfast. Source: [Amex Platinum Marriott benefits](https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/marriott-bonvoy-elite-status-benefits/).
- **Amex Platinum -> Hilton Honors Gold** (complimentary via enrollment): 80% bonus points, space-available upgrades, late checkout, **free breakfast (or equivalent credit) and 5th-night-free on award stays**. This is the more useful of the two statuses. Source: [Amex Platinum Hilton benefits](https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/hilton-honors-elite-status-benefits/).
- **CSR -> no hotel-specific elite status currently.** (Chase grants IHG Platinum via the IHG Premier card, and Hyatt Discoverist via the World of Hyatt card — the user holds neither. CSR's hotel value is the "The Edit"/portal benefits and Points Boost, not status.)

**Strategic read:** The card statuses point MR users toward **Hilton** (Gold breakfast + 5th-night-free) and **Marriott** (Gold upgrades). Neither status touches Hyatt, so Hyatt value comes purely from the award chart via UR.

### 1f. When to transfer vs book through the portal (breakeven)

Rule: **transfer to the hotel program only when (award-chart cents-per-point) > (your portal cents-per-point).**

- **CSR portal rate:** **1.5c** per point if you applied **before June 23, 2025**; **1.0c** if **after**. "Points Boost" can reach **up to 2.0c** on select flights and hotels. Sources: [AwardTravelFinder Chase value](https://awardtravelfinder.com/chase-points-value), [point.me CSR review](https://www.point.me/insights/chase-sapphire-reserve/), [TPG UR portal guide](https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/ultimate-rewards-travel-portal/).
- **Hyatt via UR:** ~1.5-2.0c. So **UR->Hyatt beats the CSR portal** whenever Hyatt prices at or above your portal rate — nearly always for a decently-valued Hyatt award, and always for a pre-June-2025 CSR holder aiming above 1.5c.
- **Marriott/IHG via UR (~0.6-0.8c):** these usually **lose** to the CSR portal at 1.5c. For Marriott/IHG, a Bay Area user is generally better off booking through the Chase portal (or paying cash) than transferring UR — unless Points Boost or a specific award is unusually rich.
- **Amex portal for prepaid hotels:** ~**0.7c** (Pay With Points), a hard floor. MR->Hilton at 1:2 typically clears this easily; MR->Marriott (1:1, ~0.8c) is roughly a wash and often not worth it.

### Single most efficient use

- **MR:** Transfer to **airline partners for premium-cabin awards** (Avianca LifeMiles for Star, Avios for Oneworld, Flying Blue for SkyTeam). For hotels specifically, **MR -> Hilton at 1:2** paired with Amex Platinum Hilton Gold. MR should almost never touch Marriott/Choice or any portal/gift-card/statement-credit path.
- **UR:** For hotels, **transfer 1:1 to World of Hyatt** — a genuinely top-tier redemption locked in by the user's CSR (which survived the Oct 1, 2026 devaluation). For flights, transfer to the same three airline anchors for premium cabins. Use Chase Travel (Points Boost / 1.5c base) only as a floor when no transfer fits.

### Where hotels fall

Hotels split into two tiers: **UR -> Hyatt is elite (rank 2)**, essentially co-headlining with premium-cabin flights and clearly beating every portal/cash path. **All other hotel transfers are mediocre-to-poor** (Hilton via MR is the one respectable exception; Marriott and IHG generally lose to simply using the Chase portal or cash).

## Caveats and freshness flags (as of 2026-09-29)

- **UR->Hyatt 1:1 for CSR is confirmed to survive** the Oct 1, 2026 devaluation; non-Reserve Chase cards drop to 4:3. Reverify your card's ratio before transferring.
- **Hyatt award chart is mid-transition.** The 5-band (Lowest/Low/Moderate/Upper/Top) chart reportedly went live May 20, 2026, but a major tracker still displayed the 3-tier chart in June 2026. Confirm live pricing on world.hyatt.com. Hyatt's official page blocked automated retrieval, so both charts here come from reputable secondary reporting, not a first-party fetch.
- **CSR portal base rate is application-date dependent** (1.5c grandfathered vs 1.0c new). Points Boost up to 2.0c is itinerary-specific and changes frequently.
- **Marriott and IHG are fully dynamic** — no fixed chart; values quoted are averages and move with demand.
- **IHG 4th-night-free requires an IHG co-brand card** the user does not hold.
- TPG's 2026 valuations (MR and UR ~2.0c) are aspirational benchmarks assuming optimal transfer use, not guaranteed on any single redemption.
