# Card Transfer Partners & Sweet Spots

Match the user's cards to the airline programs they can reach. Transferable-points banks
cannot always reach the main US carrier directly, but usually reach the alliance through
international partners.

## Amex Membership Rewards (e.g., Amex Platinum, Gold, Green)

~20 partners; deepest international airline map. Does NOT transfer to United, American, or
Alaska directly. Reaches every alliance via partners:

- SkyTeam (most direct fit): Delta (flagship co-brand), Air France/KLM Flying Blue, Virgin
  Atlantic.
- Star Alliance (via partners): Air Canada Aeroplan, Avianca LifeMiles, ANA, Singapore
  KrisFlyer. Any of these can book United flights, often cheaper than United itself.
- Oneworld (via partners): British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus (Avios), Cathay Pacific,
  Qantas.
- Non-alliance: Emirates, Etihad, Hawaiian.

Amex runs frequent, aggressive transfer bonuses (Virgin Atlantic, Flying Blue, Avianca) —
the main case where a speculative transfer can pay off.

## Chase Ultimate Rewards (e.g., Chase Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Preferred, Ink)

~14 partners; generally excellent 1:1 ratios. Reaches United directly.

- Airlines: United (direct), Air France/KLM Flying Blue, British Airways / Iberia / Aer
  Lingus (Avios), Virgin Atlantic, Singapore KrisFlyer, Southwest, JetBlue, Emirates, Air
  Canada Aeroplan.
- Hotels: World of Hyatt (exclusive, best value), Marriott, IHG.

Killer sweet spot: World of Hyatt (Category 1-4, 3,500-15,000 pts/night). Also the Chase
travel portal gives a solid value floor when transfers do not win.

## Capital One miles

~15+ partners, mostly 1:1. Includes Air Canada Aeroplan, Avianca LifeMiles, British
Airways/Avios, Flying Blue, Virgin Red, Turkish, Emirates, Singapore. No Delta, United, or
American. Reaches Star Alliance via Aeroplan/Avianca and Oneworld via Avios.

## Citi ThankYou

~15+ partners. Includes Avianca LifeMiles, Flying Blue, Turkish, Singapore, Virgin Atlantic,
Qatar, Etihad, JetBlue, Choice Hotels, Wyndham. Strong for Star and Oneworld via partners;
no US legacy carriers directly.

## Key sweet spots worth learning

- Hyatt via Chase — luxury nights for 3,500-15,000 pts.
- Avios (BA/Iberia/Aer Lingus/Qatar) — cheap short-haul (from ~4,000 Avios) and off-peak
  long-haul; reachable from Amex, Chase, Capital One, Citi.
- Virgin Atlantic — premium cabins to/from the UK; frequent transfer bonuses.
- Air France/KLM Flying Blue — Promo Rewards plus transfer bonuses can put transatlantic
  business under ~40k points.
- ANA / Singapore / Avianca LifeMiles — outsized value on Star Alliance premium cabins;
  Avianca and Aeroplan often price United flights below what United charges.
- Turkish Miles&Smiles — cheap Star Alliance domestic and short-haul awards.

## Combining cards

Holding two ecosystems (e.g., Amex + Chase) covers nearly every useful partner. Typical
division of labor: hotel points to Chase (Hyatt); airline redemptions to whichever bank
transfers to the program that has award space and an active bonus. Keep balances in both;
do not merge everything into one program.
