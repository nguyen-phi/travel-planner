## Annual credits & statement credits

### Chase Sapphire Reserve ($795)
- $300 annual travel credit (auto-applied to travel purchases)
- Up to $500/yr with "The Edit" hotel program ($250 max per transaction), from Jan 1, 2026
- $250 Chase Travel prepaid hotel credit (through Dec 31, 2026)
- $300/yr dining credit via Sapphire Exclusive Tables / OpenTable ($150 Jan–Jun + $150 Jul–Dec)
- Complimentary Apple TV+ and Apple Music (through June 22, 2027)
- $10/month Lyft credit

### Amex Platinum ($895)
- $600 hotel credit
- $400 Resy dining credit
- $200 airline fee credit (incidental fees, one airline)
- $200 Uber Cash/yr
- $300 digital entertainment credit
- $240 digital entertainment credit (monthly; Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+, NYT, WSJ)
- Up to ~$2,900+/yr in total recurring credits (requires effort to fully use)

### Capital One Venture ($95)
- $300 Capital One Travel credit (hotels/vacation rentals) — **first cardholder year only**
- Free Hertz Five Star status
- Travel accident insurance, extended warranty

### Chase Freedom Flex ($0)
- No statement credits; value is the cash-back categories
- Trip cancellation insurance, cell phone protection, extended warranty, auto rental CDW

## Notes & freshness flags (as of 2026-09-30)
- Amex Platinum fee rose to **$895** in the 2026 refresh; credit structure was also reworked.
- Centurion Lounge access tightens to **within 5 hours of departure** from July 8, 2026.
- CSR portal base redemption is **1¢** for post-June-23-2025 applicants, **1.5¢** if grandfathered; Points Boost up to 2¢.
- Chase UR → Hyatt stays **1:1 on Sapphire Reserve** after the Oct 1, 2026 cut to 4:3 on non-Reserve cards.
- Capital One Venture's $300 travel credit is **first-year only**, unlike Venture X's recurring credit.
